Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Moonsprout moon calendar and plant care mobile application, herein referred to as "Application", created by Dark Firmament Dev as a free, ad-supported service.

1. Your Data Stays on Your Device

Everything you enter in the Application (your plants, care tasks and their history, journal entries and settings) is stored only on your device. The Application has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive or have access to this data, and we do not use any analytics service of our own.

The moon calendar is calculated on your device. The Application does not ask for your location.

2. Backups and Shared Calendars

When you back up your data or share a printable calendar as a PDF, the Application creates the file on your device and opens the Android share menu. It goes only to the place you choose there, such as your own cloud storage, a messaging app or e-mail. When you restore, the Application reads the file you pick. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

3. Notifications and Widget

  • Notifications for plant care and the optional moon of the day are scheduled on your device by Android. If you allow notifications, the Application uses Android's notification permission and restores the scheduled notifications after the phone restarts. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • The home screen widget shows data the Application has prepared on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, at most once every twenty minutes a full-screen ad after you share a calendar or a backup or add a journal entry, and at most once a day a full-screen ad when the Application opens. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the Application automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with the Application and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. None of your plants, tasks or journal entries is passed to the ad SDK.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Application asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Ratings

The Application may show Google Play's own rating window or open its Google Play page. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; the Application does not see them.

6. Deleting Your Data

Because your data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in Android's settings deletes all of it. We keep no copy. Files you have shared yourself remain where they are until you delete them.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the date at the top shows the latest update.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at darkfirmamentdev@gmail.com.
