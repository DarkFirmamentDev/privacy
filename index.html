Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Pocket Budgeter mobile application (shown as Cüzdanım, Taschen-Budget, ポケット予算 and other names depending on your language), herein referred to as "Application", created by Dark Firmament Dev as a free, ad-supported service.

1. Your Financial Data Stays on Your Device

Everything you enter in the Application (transactions, accounts, categories, spending limits, recurring payments, shopping sessions and settings) is stored only on your device. The Application has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We do not collect, receive or have access to this data, and we do not use any analytics service of our own.

2. Backups

When you tap Back Up Data, the Application creates a backup file on your device and opens the Android share menu. The file goes only to the place you choose there, such as your own cloud storage or e-mail. When you restore, the Application reads the file you pick. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

3. App Lock, Widgets and Notifications

  • App lock uses your phone's own screen lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) through Android. The Application never sees your fingerprint, face data or PIN. While the lock is on, the Application's screens are hidden in the recent apps view and cannot be captured in screenshots.
  • Home screen widgets show your balance and today's spending on your home screen. While app lock is on, the widgets hide these amounts.
  • Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. They contain no amounts and are not sent through any server.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the Application automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with the Application and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID

This data is encrypted in transit. None of your financial data is passed to the ad SDK.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Application asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Deleting Your Data

Because your data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in Android's settings deletes all of it. We keep no copy. Backup files you have shared yourself remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page, and the date at the top shows the latest update.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at darkfirmament@gmail.com.
